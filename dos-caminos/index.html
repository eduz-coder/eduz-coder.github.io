Our Story

When Dos Caminos opened on Menasha Ave, Visit Manitowoc introduced it as “Manitowoc’s first authentic Nicaraguan restaurant” — a brand-new cuisine for the city.

The menu is the one Nicaraguan families grow up with: desayuno tradicional in the morning, asados estilo nica from the grill, and fritanga favorites like tacos nicas, enchiladas dobladas and repochetas — made, in the restaurant’s own words, with “the freshest ingredients… to provide the best quality and taste.”

“Deléitate con los deliciosos platillos típicos nicaragüenses, fritangas y bebidas que nos hacen únicos.”

— Dos Caminos, in its own words
